Zander Netherbrand (ザンダー・ネザーブランド) is a male English-language Virtual YouTuber affiliated with FIRST STAGE PRODUCTION EN's first generation "Avallum," alongside Gale Galleon, Cassian Floros, Lucien Lunaris, and Rosco Graves.

2. Profile

An alluring incubus who seeks freedom, power, and to live as he desires. No matter the consequences.

3. Personality

On first glance, Zander would come off as intelligent, soft-spoken, and the wine aunt of Avallum. This is partially correct. He is also loud, boisterous, willing and able to ramble and rant about the subjects he's passionate about.

In the company he's the person everyone can turn to for help, advice and support. He claims to be a diehard romantic. He was also mentioned as a flirtatious, charismatic and dashing big guy on another member's livestream.

4. Appearance

Zander has blond hair that is parted to one side. He has horns that go from purple to black on his head and fangs. His eyes are pink, with a beauty mark under his left eye. His skin tone is pale. He has one wing on the right side and wears a fur coat on the other. He has a tattoo on his upper left arm. Zander also has a thin black tail with a pink tip. The rest of his outfit is made of black and purple, with a harness over his chest and an exposed torso. He wears lace up heeled boots with bat wings decorating the top.

5. History

Background

On 19 January 2024 (JST), FIRST STAGE PRODUCTION EN announced the upcoming debut for FSP EN's first generation, Avallum, accompanied by a teaser featuring the new Liver' designs and voices. On the same day, Zander released his PV.

Debut

Avallum made their debuts on 28 January 2024 (JST) as part of the first generation of English talents at FIRST STAGE PRODUCTION EN. They released their first original song, "Crossing Fates," produced by TAKAMICHI TSUGEI, lyrics by Shown and Avallum.

2024

  • 28 March: Zander opened his channel memberships.
  • 23 November: Zander had his 3D debut and his original song, "Cold Wrists" was released.
  • 8 December: Avallum released their second original song, "SIT TIGHT".

2025

  • 15 March: Avallum released a highly anticipated group cover, "PARANOIA” from HEARTSTEEL.
  • 4 May: Zander debuted his second outfit.
  • 24 August: Zander featured in a cover of "Choose Me” by hyadain with Banzoin Hakka and VainVT.
  • 31 October: Zander featured in and hosted a part of the Unholy3nity Halloween Relay.

2026

  • 27 January: Zander released a cover of “Out of Control” by Nothing’s Carved In Stone.

9. Relationships

Zander is a member of Avallum alongside Gale Galleon, Cassian Floros, Lucien Lunaris, and Rosco Graves. He also participates in popular group dynamics such as BloodLust (with Lucien), GoldLust (with Gale), NetherGraves (with Rosco), and the Unholy Trinity (with Banzoin Hakka and InVain_VT).
